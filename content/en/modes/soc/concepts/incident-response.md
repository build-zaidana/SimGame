An **incident** is an event that threatens security: an account gets taken over, malware spreads, or data leaks. Security teams handle it in a clear order:

1. **Detect**: notice that something is wrong (an alert, a report from an employee).
2. **Contain**: stop it from spreading first. Reset the password of the broken-into account, quarantine infected devices, block the attacker's domain.
3. **Clean up**: remove the malware and the attacker's access.
4. **Recover**: bring systems and accounts back to normal.
5. **Report & learn**: write down what happened so it doesn't happen again.

**Escalate** to a senior analyst when the incident is bigger than your authority, affects many people, or you're unsure. Reporting quickly is better than staying quiet because you're afraid of being wrong.
