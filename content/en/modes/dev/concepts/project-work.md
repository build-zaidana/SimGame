Real developers rarely start by typing code. Work usually begins with a **PRD** (_Product Requirements Document_): a document describing what the client wants.

1. **Sort the requirements.** **Clear** ones can be built right away. **Ambiguous** ones ("must be fast", "nice look") are asked about until they are measurable, for example "the number appears in under 2 seconds". **Out-of-scope** ones are noted as ideas, not quietly built.
2. **Backlog.** Requirements are broken into small cards (_user stories_): "As a customer, I want … so that …". Each card gets **points** that estimate the effort.
3. **MoSCoW priorities.** **Must**, **Should**, **Could**. Do the musts first.
4. **Sprint.** A short round of work with limited team **capacity**. Cards move across the board **To Do → In Progress → Done**.
5. **Retro.** After a sprint, look at how many points were really finished (_velocity_) so the next plan is more realistic.

Surprises always happen: a production bug or a sudden request. Stay calm, handle what matters most, and never switch off tests to hit a target.
