**Personal data** is data that can point to a person: name, national ID number, phone number, address, face photo. In Indonesia, personal data is protected by **Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP)**.

Principles data analysts use every day:

- **Purpose**: data is used only for the purpose its owner agreed to. Customer data for deliveries is not automatically allowed for advertising.
- **Minimisation**: if a meeting only needs "orders per branch", do not send a list of names and phone numbers.
- **Official channels**: office data is stored and sent through office systems, not a personal email.
- **Outsiders**: requests for personal data from vendors or for a new purpose must be checked by the **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**. That is why we **escalate**.

Summary data without identities (e.g. totals per city) is usually safe to share.
