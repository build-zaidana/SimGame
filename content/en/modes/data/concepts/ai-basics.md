**AI** (artificial intelligence) learns patterns from **training data**. That is why data analysts help decide whether an AI is fair and safe.

- **Bias**: if 90% of the job-application training data comes from men, an AI that screens applications tends to rate women lower. Training data must represent every group it will serve.
- **Hallucination**: language-based AI assistants can write convincing answers, complete with a "regulation number" that does not exist. Check the original source before using it.
- **Privacy**: never paste customer data or office secrets into a public AI service. The data may be stored outside the office's control.
- **Consent**: using personal data to train AI needs a lawful basis. If it is unclear, **escalate**.

A well-checked AI is a great help. An AI answer that matches the source data can be approved.
