A good technician knows **their limits**. **Escalating** means handing a case to a team with special access or expertise, while writing down what you've already tried.

Escalate when:

- It needs special access: servers, licenses, or the central network.
- **There are signs of an attack**: unknown programs appear, the antivirus turns itself off, files suddenly get locked, or the cursor moves by itself. Disconnect it from the network (unplug the cable or turn off Wi-Fi), **don't shut it down or delete anything**, then hand it to the **SOC** team. The traces on that device are important evidence.
- **Important data** sits on a dying disk. Don't experiment; the data recovery team has special tools.

But don't escalate everything. Simple problems that can be guided or fixed at the desk only keep specialists busy.

**Safety first**: a burning smell, smoke, or spilled liquid → unplug the power and don't switch it on again.
