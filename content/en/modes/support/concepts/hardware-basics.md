**Hardware** is the part of a computer you can touch:

- **RAM**: working memory. Loose RAM can make a laptop beep and show nothing.
- **Disk** (HDD/SSD): where files are stored. A repeated "click" sound or many _bad sectors_ = a dying disk.
- **Battery**: the laptop's power source. Health below 50% means it drains fast.
- **Fan & heatsink**: the cooling. Thick dust makes a laptop overheat and shut itself down.

When to **Fix**, when to **Replace Part**?

- Loose, dirty, dusty → just **fix** it: reseat or clean it, then test again.
- Cracked, burnt, swollen, or failing tests → **replace** the part.

A **swollen battery** is dangerous because it can catch fire. Don't charge it, don't press it, replace it right away.

Before opening the case, **shut down and unplug the power**. Touch metal or wear an antistatic wrist strap so static electricity from your body doesn't damage parts.
