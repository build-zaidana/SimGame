**Troubleshooting** means finding the cause of a problem step by step, not guessing.

1. **Gather symptoms.** When did it start? What changed before? Can it be repeated?
2. **Check the simplest things first.** Power, cables, buttons, then a restart. Many "breakdowns" turn out to be an unplugged cable.
3. **Look at the evidence.** Error messages, indicator lights, and system readings are more reliable than rumors.
4. **Change one thing at a time**, then test again. If you change many things at once, you won't know which one worked.
5. **Write down** the cause and the fix in the ticket.

The IT Workshop has four decisions:

- **Guide User**: the device is healthy; just teach the user.
- **Fix**: something is wrong, but it can be sorted out without a new part.
- **Replace Part**: a part is physically broken.
- **Escalate**: it needs special access or expertise.
